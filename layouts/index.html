OUR PHILOSOPHY

The genome is more than a repository of genetic information.

Over evolutionary time, genomes have acquired multiple layers of organization that shape the range of biological possibilities available to a cell. We seek to uncover how these layers are established, interpreted, and rewired during development and disease.
