Work with us.

We welcome curious and collaborative researchers who want to understand how genome organization shapes cellular possibility in development and disease.

01

Research directions

Repetitive genome & chromatin

How repetitive DNA contributes to nucleosome organization, chromatin identity, and higher-order genome architecture.

O-GlcNAc & nuclear organization

How metabolism-sensitive protein modification regulates IDRs, biomolecular condensates, and cell-state transitions.

Endogenous retroviruses & cancer

How latent retroviral programs are reactivated and repurposed to reshape tumor cell identity and therapeutic response.

02

Opportunities

Postdoctoral Fellows

We welcome applicants with strong training in cell biology, genetics, biochemistry, genomics, computational biology, cancer biology, or related areas. We especially value people who want to develop an independent scientific question while contributing to the broader intellectual direction of the group.

Graduate Students

Prospective PhD and MSc students interested in genome organization, cell-state plasticity, or disease biology are encouraged to contact us. Formal admission follows the graduate-program procedures of Central South University.

Research Staff & Visiting Researchers

We also welcome inquiries from research assistants, technical staff, visiting students, and researchers whose expertise can complement ongoing projects in the lab.

03

What we value

We value scientific curiosity, rigor, openness, and independence. Our projects often cross conventional boundaries between genetics, cell biology, biochemistry, genomics, development, and disease, and we encourage members to learn from one another and to follow unexpected observations when they lead to interesting questions.

04

How to apply

Please send a CV and a brief description of your research interests and relevant experience to yuankai@csu.edu.cn. You may also include representative publications or other material that helps us understand your previous work and the questions you hope to pursue.
