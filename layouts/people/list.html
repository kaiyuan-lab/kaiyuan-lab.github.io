Principal Investigator

Kai Yuan, PhD

Professor · Principal Investigator

Xiangya Hospital, Central South University

Email Google Scholar ORCID

Kai Yuan trained in chromosome biology with Xuebiao Yao at the University of Science and Technology of China and in developmental biology with Patrick O’Farrell at the University of California, San Francisco. He established the Yuan Group in 2017. His research seeks to understand how hierarchical genome organization shapes cellular possibility during development and disease.
